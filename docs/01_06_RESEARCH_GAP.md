# Phase 1.6 — Research gap identification

**Status: working questions, not established literature gaps.**

Potential comparisons worth checking against published work:

- A simple spectral/Hjorth baseline compared fairly against raw-signal CNN, LSTM and Transformer under the **same participant split**.
- How much performance is sensitive to selecting Fpz–Cz alone vs both EEG channels?
- Is N1 classification particularly difficult and does a fixed-weight ensemble help, under the same benchmark?
- Do input-gradient saliency maps emphasize medically plausible transient events, and what artifacts could fool those maps?

## To verify before claiming a gap

1. Read papers from the last five years, note datasets, splits and channels.
2. Identify what has *not* been tested under comparable conditions.
3. Replicate a simple baseline before proposing modifications.
4. Mark the distinction between an **engineering comparison** and a novel research contribution.
5. Ask a supervisor to review feasibility and novelty.

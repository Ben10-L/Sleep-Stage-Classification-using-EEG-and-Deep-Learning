# Research integrity and honest reporting

- Code readiness and research completion are different statuses.
- Synthetic data illustrate APIs only. No clinical/benchmark claims may be made from them.
- Never distribute raw EDF data or annotated subject records in Git.
- Do not imply that this project replicates published models exactly. The networks are lightweight teaching baselines.
- Real-data numbers must include dataset source, subset, sample count, subject IDs or split specification, training hyperparameters, and test metrics.
- Do not present unrun cells, placeholder tables, or mock performance figures as observations.
- Model saliency is not a physiological explanation or causal proof.
- Disclose relevant use of AI assistance if required by your institution or journal. Authorship should reflect work you have genuinely understood, reviewed and performed.
